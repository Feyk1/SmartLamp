      Simulation guide

      Pairing

      1. Panel Sim → Device: Power Off, wait 5–10 s, Power On and wait for APP_RUNNING.
      2. Repeat the off→on cycle 3 times. The third one enters pairing; a fourth exits.
      3. The dashboard Turn off/on is soft power and does not count. Use Add device to connect.

      Soft vs hard power

      Turn off/on in the dashboard is soft and keeps Wi-Fi. Power Off/On in Panel Sim is hard and counts for pairing.

      Network policy

      Edit valid SSIDs and passwords, enable Allow any, or simulate wrong password, unknown network or timeout.
